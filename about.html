About the project

Legal information people can actually understand.

RightsRadar UK is being built as an independent legal-information service. Its goal is to translate complex public information into clear explanations without hiding the jurisdiction, source or date behind the answer.

Our publishing rules

Guides should identify the relevant UK jurisdiction, link to authoritative sources, show when they were last checked and clearly separate general information from personalised legal advice.

What we are not

We are not a law firm, a government service, the police or a court. The website should never replace a solicitor when someone needs advice about their own case.
