Abortion-service safe access zones across the UK

All three UK legal systems now restrict specified protest, influencing, obstruction or harassment around premises providing abortion services, but the laws and zone sizes are different.

Core pointDo not assume the same distance or wording applies everywhere. England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland use separate legislation.

England & Wales

Safe access zones came into force on 31 October 2024. A 150-metre zone applies around qualifying abortion clinics and hospitals. Within the zone it is an offence to intentionally or recklessly influence a person’s decision to access or facilitate abortion services, obstruct access, or cause harassment, alarm or distress connected with access, provision or facilitation. The maximum penalty is an unlimited fine.

Scotland

Safe Access Zones came into force on 24 September 2024 under the Abortion Services (Safe Access Zones) (Scotland) Act 2024. The default zone covers the premises, public grounds and public areas within 200 metres. Prohibited conduct includes intentional or reckless influencing, impeding access, or causing harassment, alarm or distress. Scottish Government guidance notes that conduct such as leafleting, preaching or silent vigils can fall within the law depending on intent, recklessness and circumstances.

Northern Ireland

Safe Access Zones were introduced at abortion-service locations from 29 September 2023 under the Abortion Services (Safe Access Zones) Act (Northern Ireland) 2023. The zone includes the protected premises and designated public areas generally set between 100 and 250 metres from entrances and exits. The law prohibits intentional or reckless conduct that will or might influence a protected person, prevent or impede access, or harass, alarm or distress them.

Does this ban protest everywhere?

No. These laws restrict specified conduct inside defined zones around protected services. Protest and expression outside the zones remain subject to the ordinary law, including public-order, harassment, obstruction and other relevant offences.
