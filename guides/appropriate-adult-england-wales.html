What is an Appropriate Adult?

PACE Code C provides extra safeguards for children and vulnerable people dealing with police. An Appropriate Adult is there to support, advise and assist, and to help safeguard the person’s rights and welfare.

Core pointAn Appropriate Adult is not a replacement for a solicitor. A person may need both.

Who may need one?

People under 18 and adults who are treated as vulnerable under PACE safeguards may require an Appropriate Adult for interviews and other procedures.

What does the Appropriate Adult do?

The role includes helping the person understand what is happening, supporting communication, observing whether police act properly and fairly, and helping protect rights and welfare during procedures.

Who can act as one?

Depending on the person, this may be a parent, guardian, carer, social worker or another responsible adult who meets the independence requirements in PACE Code C.

Can my solicitor be my Appropriate Adult?

The roles are different. PACE guidance makes clear that an Appropriate Adult safeguards welfare and procedural rights, while a solicitor gives legal advice.
