What is an Appropriate Adult in Northern Ireland?

Children and vulnerable adults may need an Appropriate Adult to support them when dealing with police in custody.

Core pointAn Appropriate Adult helps protect rights, supports communication and can intervene if necessary. They are not a substitute for a solicitor.

What can they do?

PSNI guidance says an Appropriate Adult can speak privately with the detainee, inspect the custody record where practicable, be present for key procedures, ask for breaks and help communication during interview.

Can they give legal advice?

No. Their role is support and safeguarding. A solicitor gives legal advice.

Can they request a solicitor?

PSNI guidance says an Appropriate Adult can ask for a solicitor if they believe legal advice is in the detained person's best interests.

Official source used

PSNI — Guidance for Appropriate Adults

Legal information, not legal adviceIf you are unsure whether an Appropriate Adult is required, raise it with the custody officer and solicitor immediately.
