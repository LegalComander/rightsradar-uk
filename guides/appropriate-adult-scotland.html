What is an Appropriate Adult in Scotland?

An Appropriate Adult can help some people understand what is happening and communicate with police. The service is separate from legal advice.

Ask if you need communication supportScottish Government guidance says an Appropriate Adult can help you understand what police are saying and doing, and can help explain questions if you do not understand them.

Who might need an Appropriate Adult?

The service is intended for people who need help understanding what is happening or communicating effectively. If you think you need this support, tell the police.

Can they give me legal advice?

No. The Scottish Government guidance is clear that an Appropriate Adult cannot give legal advice. You can have support from an Appropriate Adult and still speak to a solicitor.

Do I have to ask first?

The guidance says police should call an Appropriate Adult if they think you need one, even if you do not ask.

What should I say?

If you are struggling to understand, say so clearly and ask the police to arrange an Appropriate Adult. Also ask for a solicitor if you need legal advice.
