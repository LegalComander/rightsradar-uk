When can police arrest me in England and Wales?

This page gives a short introduction to ordinary police arrest powers. Different powers can apply in specialist situations.

Core pointGOV.UK says police need reasonable grounds to suspect you are involved in a crime and your arrest must be necessary.

What must police tell me?

If you are arrested, police must identify themselves, tell you that you are under arrest, tell you the offence they suspect, explain why the arrest is necessary and make clear that you are not free to leave.

Can police arrest me anywhere?

GOV.UK says police can exercise arrest powers at any time and in places including the street, your home or work, subject to the law governing entry and the particular circumstances.

Can police use force?

Police may use reasonable force where the law allows it. What is reasonable depends on the circumstances.

What happens next?

You will usually be taken to a police station, where custody safeguards apply. Ask for free legal advice as soon as possible.
