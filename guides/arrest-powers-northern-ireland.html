When can police arrest me in Northern Ireland?

The Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 and PACE Code G govern important arrest powers and safeguards.

Core pointAn arrest must have a lawful basis. Police should tell you that you are under arrest and the grounds for the arrest as soon as reasonably practicable.

What happens after arrest?

You will normally be taken to a police station or other authorised place where a custody officer considers whether detention should continue and records your custody.

Can force be used?

Police powers can include reasonable force where the law permits it. Whether force was lawful depends on the circumstances and proportionality.

What rights follow?

Key custody rights include legal advice, having someone informed of your arrest, access to the applicable PACE Code, and safeguards during questioning.
