When can police arrest me in Scotland?

Police in Scotland need a lawful basis to arrest you. Official guidance says they may arrest where they have reasonable grounds to suspect involvement in a crime, where there is enough evidence to charge, where a warrant exists, or in certain other situations.

What should police tell me?

Official guidance says police should identify themselves, tell you that you are being arrested, tell you the crime they think you committed, explain why arrest is necessary, and tell you about your right not to answer questions beyond required personal details.

Can police arrest me at home or work?

Yes. The Scottish guidance says police powers of arrest can be used anywhere and at any time, including on the street, at home or at work.

Can police search me after arrest?

Police have powers to search a person after arrest. Separate rules govern samples, intimate searches and other procedures.
