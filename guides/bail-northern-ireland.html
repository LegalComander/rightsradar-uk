Bail after charge in Northern Ireland

After charge, a person may be released on police bail or kept in custody for the first available court to consider bail.

Core pointThe court considers risks such as absconding, interference with witnesses, further offending, perverting justice and public-order concerns when deciding whether detention is necessary.

Can bail have conditions?

Yes. Conditions can include residence requirements, curfews, restrictions on places or people, alcohol restrictions and electronic monitoring where lawful and appropriate.

Can I apply again if bail is refused?

Further applications may be possible, particularly where circumstances have changed. Your solicitor can advise on the correct route.

What if I breach bail?

Breaching conditions can have serious consequences, including arrest and a renewed decision about custody. Get legal advice immediately if you think a breach has occurred.
