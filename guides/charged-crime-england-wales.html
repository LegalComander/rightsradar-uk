Charged with a crime in England & Wales

This starter guide explains what GOV.UK says normally happens after a person is charged.

Core pointIf you are charged, you are given a charge sheet setting out the offence. Police then decide whether you can be released until court, usually on bail, or kept in custody until your hearing.

Where is the first hearing?

GOV.UK says the first court hearing after charge is at a magistrates’ court, even where the case may later go to the Crown Court.

Can I be released before court?

Yes. Police may release you on bail, sometimes with conditions. In other cases you can be kept in custody and taken to court.

What about minor offences?

Some minor offences can use the single justice procedure instead of a normal courtroom hearing. If you receive a notice, read it carefully and respond within the stated deadline.
