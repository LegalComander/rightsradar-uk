Charged with a crime in Northern Ireland

If a prosecution begins by charge sheet, police charge the arrested suspect and the Public Prosecution Service reviews the charge before the first court appearance.

Core pointAfter charge, you may be held for the first available court or released on police bail to attend court on a specified date.

What does the PPS do?

The Public Prosecution Service reviews the evidence and decides whether prosecution should continue and how the case should proceed.

What if I am kept in custody?

If held after charge, you must be brought before the first available court so the court can consider bail and the next steps.

What if I am released?

You may be released on police bail with a court date. Read all bail conditions carefully and get legal advice before taking any action that might breach them.
