How to complain about police in Northern Ireland

The Police Ombudsman's Office for Northern Ireland is independent of the police and investigates complaints about police conduct.

Core pointYou can complain about police conduct ranging from rudeness or poor behaviour to allegations of excessive force or serious criminality.

Is there a time limit?

The Police Ombudsman says complaints should normally be made within one year of the incident, although older matters can sometimes be investigated where the issues are grave or exceptional.

What evidence should I keep?

Keep dates, times, locations, officer names or shoulder numbers, witness details and any photographs, audio or video.

How do I complain?

The Police Ombudsman provides an online complaint form and telephone contact. Complaints are assessed and investigated according to the applicable rules.
