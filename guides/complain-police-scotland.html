How do I complain about Police Scotland?

If you believe Police Scotland did not provide the service you expected or acted unfairly or incorrectly, you can make a complaint. If you are unhappy with how Police Scotland handled that complaint, there is an independent review route.

Where do I make the first complaint?

mygov.scot directs people to make complaints through Police Scotland.

What if I am unhappy with the outcome?

You can contact the Police Investigations and Review Commissioner (PIRC), which is independent of the police, to ask for a Complaint Handling Review.

Is there a time limit?

The mygov.scot page updated 17 June 2026 says applications to PIRC should be sent within 3 months of Police Scotland sending its findings, unless exceptional circumstances justify lateness.
