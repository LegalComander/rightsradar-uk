Criminal legal aid in Northern Ireland

Criminal legal aid can pay for a solicitor and, where appropriate, a barrister in criminal proceedings.

Core pointFor court criminal legal aid, the court considers financial circumstances and whether granting legal aid is in the interests of justice. Applications are normally handled with a solicitor.

Which courts are covered?

Criminal legal aid can apply to cases in the Magistrates' Courts, Crown Court and Court of Appeal.

What about police-station advice?

Department of Justice guidance describes free advice and assistance for a person being interviewed at a police station in connection with a suspected offence.

How do I apply?

Speak to a solicitor. nidirect says a solicitor is the best person to explain whether you may qualify and to deal with the application process.
