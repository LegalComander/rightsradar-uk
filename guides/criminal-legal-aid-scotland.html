Can I get criminal legal aid in Scotland?

Criminal legal aid can help with the cost of a solicitor and court representation if you are charged with a criminal offence and meet the relevant tests.

Is criminal legal aid automatic?

No. The current Scottish guidance says financial circumstances are considered, and different tests can apply depending on the court and seriousness of the case.

What kinds of criminal cases can qualify?

mygov.scot says criminal legal aid is commonly available for serious cases, certain summary cases and criminal appeals. In Justice of the Peace court cases, additional factors such as risk of imprisonment, loss of employment or difficulty following proceedings may matter.

How do I apply?

Speak to a solicitor who does legal-aid work. Your solicitor can advise whether you are likely to qualify and handle the application.
