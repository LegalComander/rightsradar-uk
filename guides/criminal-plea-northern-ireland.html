Guilty or not guilty plea in Northern Ireland

Your plea is a major decision in a criminal case and should be made only after understanding the allegation, evidence and legal consequences.

Core pointIn the Magistrates' Court, a guilty plea leads to conviction and sentencing, either immediately or at a later hearing. A not-guilty plea leads toward a contested hearing or trial.

What happens after a guilty plea?

The court moves to sentence. It may sentence on the day or adjourn for reports or further information.

What happens after a not-guilty plea?

The case is prepared for a contested hearing or trial, with evidence and witnesses considered by the court.

Can timing matter?

Yes. Plea timing can affect procedure and potentially sentence. Do not rely on general internet guidance for that decision — get advice on your particular case.
