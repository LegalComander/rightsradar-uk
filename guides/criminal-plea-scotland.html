What does guilty or not guilty mean at a Scottish criminal court?

At the start of a criminal case, the accused is asked to plead to the charge or charges. A plea has major consequences, so take legal advice before deciding what to do.

What happens after a not-guilty plea?

The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service says a trial date will be fixed so evidence can be heard. In summary cases, an intermediate diet is normally set before trial to check the case is ready.

Can someone plead guilty later?

Official court guidance says an accused person can plead guilty at any stage in the proceedings.

Should I decide my plea without a solicitor?

A plea can affect conviction, sentence and trial rights. If you are accused of a crime, get advice from a Scottish criminal solicitor before entering a plea.
