Can I tell someone I am in police custody?

Yes. GOV.UK lists telling someone where you are as one of the main rights you must be told about in police custody.

Core pointYou have the right to have someone told where you are. The detailed timing and any lawful delay are governed by PACE and the custody codes.

Who can be told?

This is usually a friend, relative or another person you want informed. If you are under 18, police must also try to contact your parent, guardian or carer.

Is this the same as making a phone call?

Not necessarily. The custody right is about having someone informed that you are detained. Access to calls and communication can depend on custody rules and the circumstances.

Can informing someone ever be delayed?

PACE allows limited delay in specified circumstances. If this happens, the reason and authorisation should be recorded. Ask your solicitor if you are unsure why contact has been delayed.
