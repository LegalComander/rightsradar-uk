Can I tell someone I am in police custody?

PACE Code C gives a person arrested and held in custody the right, on request, to have someone known to them or likely to take an interest in their welfare informed of their whereabouts.

Core pointYou can ask for someone to be told where you are. PSNI says this should happen as soon as practicable, subject to limited lawful grounds for delay.

What if the first person cannot be reached?

PSNI guidance says alternatives can be nominated if the first person cannot be contacted.

Can I speak to them myself?

PSNI says detainees are generally facilitated in speaking with people during detention where appropriate, although communication can sometimes be delayed or restricted for lawful investigative reasons.
