Can I tell someone I am in police custody in Scotland?

Scottish custody guidance includes a right to have someone told that you are at the police station.

You can ask for someone to be toldThe Scottish Government says police will ask who you would like them to contact. This could be a family member, partner, carer, friend or another person you know.

Can I phone them myself?

The official guidance says you have a right to have someone told you are at the police station, but you might not be allowed to speak to that person directly.

Who can I choose?

The guidance gives examples including a family member, partner, carer or friend.

Is this the same as contacting a solicitor?

No. Your right to legal advice is separate. You can ask for a solicitor to be contacted and can speak to a solicitor privately.

What if I am under 18?

There are additional protections for children and some 16- and 17-year-olds, including rules about notifying or involving a parent or guardian. The exact protection depends on age and legal status.
