Your rights in police custody in Northern Ireland

PACE Code C governs the detention, treatment and questioning of people held by police under the ordinary PACE framework.

Core pointCustody rights include access to independent legal advice, having someone informed of your arrest, consulting the Code of Practice, reasonable physical comfort, food and drink, toilet and washing facilities, medical attention and other safeguards.

Who oversees detention?

A custody officer is responsible for key decisions about detention and for recording important events on the custody record.

What about interviews?

Police questioning is subject to PACE safeguards. Ask for a solicitor before answering questions if you want legal advice.

What if I need support?

Children and vulnerable adults may require an appropriate adult. Healthcare and communication support should also be considered where needed.
