How long can police hold you in custody?

This starter guide summarises the main custody time limits stated by GOV.UK for England and Wales.

Core pointPolice can normally hold you for up to 24 hours before they must charge you or release you.

Can the 24-hour limit be extended?

Yes in some serious cases. GOV.UK says police can apply to hold a suspect for up to 36 hours or up to 96 hours where a serious crime is suspected.

What about terrorism arrests?

GOV.UK states that a person arrested under terrorism legislation can be held without charge for up to 14 days.

What if police do not charge me?

You may be released. Depending on the circumstances, this can include release on police bail or release under investigation while enquiries continue.

Does police bail mean I have to pay money?

No. GOV.UK says you do not pay money to be released on police bail. You may have to return to the police station and conditions can apply in some cases.
