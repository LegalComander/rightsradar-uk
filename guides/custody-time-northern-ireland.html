How long can police hold me in Northern Ireland?

Under the ordinary PACE framework, the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 sets a general limit on detention without charge.

Core pointThe ordinary starting limit is 24 hours without charge. The law allows extensions in specified circumstances and under additional authorisation and court safeguards.

Does the clock always start at arrest?

No. The legislation uses a defined “relevant time”, so the calculation can depend on the circumstances of the arrest and arrival at a police station.

Can detention go beyond 24 hours?

Yes, for qualifying investigations and where statutory tests are met. Longer detention requires additional authorisation; the exact route depends on the offence and circumstances.

Are terrorism cases different?

Yes. Terrorism and national-security detention have separate statutory regimes and PACE Codes H and I. Do not use the ordinary 24-hour rule as the whole answer in those cases.
