Fingerprints, photographs and DNA in Northern Ireland

PACE NI and Code D regulate important police identification procedures, including fingerprints, photographs and body samples.

Core pointPolice have statutory powers to take certain fingerprints and non-intimate samples in specified circumstances without consent. Other procedures have different consent and authorisation rules.

What is Code D for?

Code D covers identification procedures and safeguards, including fingerprints, photographs, identification procedures and samples used for comparison and identification.

Can material be retained?

Retention rules depend on the statutory basis, the offence, whether a person was charged or convicted, age and other factors. The rules have been the subject of recent reform work in Northern Ireland, so do not rely on a simple one-rule summary.

What should I do if I dispute the taking or retention?

Ask which legal power is being used and get advice from a solicitor about the specific material and retention regime.
