What is investigative liberation in Scotland?

Police can sometimes release a suspect while continuing an investigation and impose temporary conditions. This is called investigative liberation.

How long can conditions last?

mygov.scot says investigative liberation conditions cannot last longer than 28 days.

Can I appeal the conditions?

Yes. The official guidance says you have a right to appeal to a sheriff court if you do not agree with the conditions.

What if I break a condition?

Breaking a condition of investigative liberation is a criminal offence and can lead to arrest.
