Legal advice at a police station in Northern Ireland

People detained under PACE have a right to consult privately with a solicitor. Department of Justice guidance describes police-station advice in connection with a suspected offence as free advice and assistance.

Core pointYou can ask for a solicitor at any stage of detention. Legal advice is independent of the police.

Can I speak privately?

PACE safeguards provide for private consultation with a solicitor. A solicitor may advise you before and during questioning.

Should I wait for a solicitor?

Whether questioning can proceed before legal advice depends on the applicable rules and circumstances. If you want advice, say so clearly to the custody officer.

What if I am young or vulnerable?

An appropriate adult is additional support and does not replace legal advice.
