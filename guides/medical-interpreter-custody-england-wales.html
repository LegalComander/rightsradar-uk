Medical help, interpreters and communication support in custody

Police custody rules include safeguards for health, language and communication needs.

Core pointGOV.UK lists medical help as a custody right and says you can ask for your written rights in your language or for an interpreter to explain them.

What if I feel ill or need medication?

Tell the custody officer immediately about illness, injury, prescribed medication, mental-health concerns or other medical needs. Police custody procedures include access to appropriate healthcare where needed.

What if I do not understand English?

You can ask for an interpreter. PACE Code C contains detailed rules about interpretation during custody and interviews so that important information can be understood.

What if I have a hearing, speech or communication difficulty?

PACE requires police to take communication needs seriously. Depending on your circumstances this can include interpretation, communication assistance and Appropriate Adult safeguards.

Can police interview me if I am unwell?

Fitness for detention and interview is fact-sensitive. Raise health concerns immediately and ask for legal advice. Healthcare professionals and custody staff may need to assess whether an interview should proceed.
