Medical help, interpreters and communication support in custody

People in police custody must be treated safely and fairly. Health and communication needs can affect whether someone can properly participate in detention and questioning.

Core pointPSNI says detainees are risk-assessed on booking and can be assessed by healthcare professionals where needed. Communication needs should also be raised immediately.

What medical help is available?

PSNI says custody healthcare professionals can assess detainees, provide some treatment in the police station and refer people to external medical facilities where necessary.

What if I do not understand English well?

Tell the custody officer. PACE safeguards require procedures to be understood, and interpretation or other communication support may be needed.

What if I have a disability or communication difficulty?

Raise it as early as possible. An Appropriate Adult or other support may be required depending on vulnerability and communication needs.
