Medical help, interpreters and communication support in Scottish police custody

Being in police custody does not remove your right to basic support. Scottish official guidance identifies several practical rights that matter if you are ill or have difficulty understanding what is happening.

Medical help

mygov.scot says you have a right to medical help if you are feeling ill while in custody.

Interpreter

If you do not speak or understand English, official guidance says you have a right to an interpreter.

Communication support

If you are deaf or have difficulty understanding what is happening, you can ask for communication help. Vulnerable adults may also qualify for Appropriate Adult support.

Letter of Rights

You should be able to see the written Letter of Rights, including in another language or easy-read form where available, and police can read it to you if needed.
