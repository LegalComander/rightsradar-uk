Police questioning and interviews in Northern Ireland

PACE Code C governs detention and questioning, while Codes E and F cover recording of suspect interviews.

Core pointIf you are interviewed as a suspect, what you say or do not say can matter. Ask for independent legal advice before deciding how to answer questions.

Are interviews recorded?

PACE includes rules for audio recording and, in relevant circumstances, visual recording with sound of suspect interviews.

Can I have a solicitor?

Yes. A detained person can ask to consult privately with a solicitor. This is a continuing right during detention.

What if I need communication support?

Children and vulnerable adults may require an appropriate adult, and interpreters or other communication support may be needed where a person cannot understand or communicate effectively.
