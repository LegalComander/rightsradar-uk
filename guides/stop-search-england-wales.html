Stopped and searched by police in England & Wales

This starter guide explains the basic position in plain English and links to the official GOV.UK guidance for the full rules.

Core pointPolice can stop and question you at any time, but whether they can search you depends on the circumstances and the legal power being used.

Can police stop and question me?

Yes. GOV.UK says police can stop and question you at any time. That does not automatically mean they have a power to search you.

Can police search me?

Sometimes. The power to search depends on the situation and the legal authority the officer is relying on. The rules are not the same as in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

What if I think I was treated unfairly?

Keep a note of what happened, including the time, place and anything the officer told you. GOV.UK links to the official route for complaining about police treatment.

Should I rely only on this page?

No. Stop-and-search powers vary by circumstances. Check the official source and get legal advice if the search is connected to an arrest, evidence, a charge or an active case.
