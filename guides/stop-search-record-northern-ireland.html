How to get your stop-and-search record in Northern Ireland

PSNI says that where a constable has made a record of a stop and search, the person searched is entitled to request a copy.

Core pointPSNI says you can ask for a copy within 12 months of the search.

How do I request it?

PSNI says you can attend a police station and request the record in person.

What information helps?

Bring as much identifying information as you can, such as the date and place of the search, the officer's police service number or any reference number you were given.

What if I want to complain?

Getting the record and making a complaint are separate steps. If you believe the officer acted improperly, you can also contact the Police Ombudsman for Northern Ireland.
