Can I get a record of a stop and search in Scotland?

Police Scotland says that if you were stopped and searched, you may be entitled to a copy of the stop-and-search record.

How long do I have to ask?

Police Scotland says you are entitled to request your stop-and-search information within six months of the search date.

Are there exceptions?

Yes. Police Scotland lists situations where stop-and-search information is not available through this route, including some searches where identifying details were not provided, searches after arrest, and certain other statutory searches.

What if I was unhappy with how the search was carried out?

You can make a complaint about police treatment separately from requesting the search record.
