What is a police undertaking in Scotland?

An undertaking lets a person who has been charged leave police custody before their first court hearing, subject to conditions.

What conditions usually apply?

Official guidance lists conditions such as attending court, not committing offences, and not interfering with witnesses or evidence. Extra conditions can also be imposed, for example staying at or away from certain places.

Can I challenge extra conditions?

mygov.scot says you can appeal further undertaking conditions to a court. The procurator fiscal may also revoke or relax conditions separately from an appeal.

What if I break the conditions?

Breaching undertaking conditions can lead to arrest and further consequences. Get legal advice immediately if you think you may have breached a condition.
