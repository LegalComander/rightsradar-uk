Young and vulnerable people in police custody

PACE safeguards provide additional protection where a detained person is a child or a vulnerable adult.

Core pointAn Appropriate Adult may be required to support communication and safeguard the detained person's rights during important custody procedures.

When should an Appropriate Adult be present?

PSNI guidance describes their role during rights and entitlements, cautions, interviews, charging, certain searches, detention reviews, identification procedures, fingerprints, photographs and sampling, subject to limited exceptions.

Does an Appropriate Adult replace a solicitor?

No. Legal advice and Appropriate Adult support are different safeguards and can both be needed.

What if the person struggles to understand?

Tell the custody officer immediately about learning disability, mental-health difficulty, communication needs or anything else that may affect understanding or participation.
