Privacy notice

This notice explains how RightsRadar UK handles information when you use this website. RightsRadar UK is an independent legal-information project. For privacy questions, account-data requests or deletion requests, email ukrightsradar@gmail.com.

Community accounts

If you create a RightsRadar community account, Neon Auth stores authentication information such as your email address, display name, password credential data in protected form, account identifiers and session information in the connected Neon PostgreSQL database. RightsRadar UK does not receive or store your plain-text password.

When you sign in, RightsRadar sets a strictly necessary secure, HttpOnly session cookie so the site can recognise your authenticated session. This cookie is used for account security and community functionality, not advertising.

Your chosen display name, community posts, comments and member-chat messages are visible to other Community users as applicable. Member profiles can also show your display name, account join date, public contribution counts and the jurisdiction preference you choose to save. Your account email address is not displayed on public member profiles or community posts.

RightsRadar may also store which discussions and topics you follow, helpful votes you give or receive, notification records and read/unread state, moderation reports, and your saved Community jurisdiction preference. Follow lists and notification history are used to provide the member features and are not published as a public list. Reports you submit for moderation are not displayed publicly.

Please do not post private case documents, addresses, phone numbers, medical information or other sensitive personal information in the Community or member chat. Community content is intended for general discussion and is not a private channel for legal advice.

Law Alerts beta

If you successfully register for the Law Alerts beta, RightsRadar UK stores the email address you provide together with your chosen jurisdiction, topics, alert-frequency preference and the time you gave consent. These details are used to operate and develop the Law Alerts service. The signup does not ask for details about a legal case.

Law Alerts registration data is stored server-side in PostgreSQL through Neon and connected to the RightsRadar UK Vercel project. Automated email delivery is not live yet, and no paid Law Alerts subscription is offered until delivery is connected.

Installed app, saved pages and device-only settings

RightsRadar can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA). The PWA service worker may keep copies of public RightsRadar pages and interface files in your browser cache so the app can load faster and selected material can remain available when your connection is poor.

If you choose a default legal system, save a page, change text size, enable high contrast or reduce motion, those choices are stored in browser storage on that device. The Saved page and these app settings do not require an account and RightsRadar does not send those local preferences to the server merely because you use them.

Saved-page entries normally contain the RightsRadar page address, its title and the time you saved it. Clearing the browser’s site data, storage or cache can remove saved pages, app preferences and offline copies.

The read-aloud control uses your browser or operating system speech feature where supported. RightsRadar does not upload the page text to a RightsRadar speech service when using that built-in control.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting services, Vercel may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page, time and security-related logs.

Cookies, consent and advertising

Strictly necessary account/session cookies may be used without being used for advertising. RightsRadar UK is preparing Google AdSense and has selected Google’s consent-management platform for UK/EEA consent. Advertising is not treated as active merely because the site is under AdSense review. Consent controls and this notice will be kept aligned before non-essential advertising storage or personalised advertising is enabled.

Live law feed

The New Laws and Community news areas ask the RightsRadar UK server to retrieve public information from legislation.gov.uk and the UK Parliament. Those public feeds are not sent your account email address, community session or Law Alerts preferences.

Why information is used and how long it is kept

Account and community information is processed to provide the account, discussion, chat, profile, follow, helpful-vote, notification and moderation features you choose to use, and to protect the service against abuse. Law Alerts beta information is processed because you actively ask to register and consent to the storage of the preferences shown on the form. Technical hosting and security information may be processed where necessary for the legitimate interests of operating, securing and diagnosing the website.

Account, community and Law Alerts information can be removed where appropriate following a valid deletion request, subject to information that may need to be retained for security, moderation, legal obligations or dispute handling. Infrastructure-log retention is controlled through the hosting service and its applicable settings. Device-only PWA settings remain under your browser/device control and can normally be removed by clearing RightsRadar site data.

Sharing and service providers

RightsRadar UK does not sell community-account or Law Alerts registration data. Vercel and Neon may process information where needed to host the website, authenticate users and store database records. Future email-delivery, push-notification and payment providers will be identified here when those services are activated.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Visit ico.org.uk for the official complaint route.

Changes to this notice

This page will be updated when the website’s data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
