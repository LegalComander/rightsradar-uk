Privacy notice

This notice explains how RightsRadar UK handles information when you use this website. RightsRadar UK is an independent legal-information project. For privacy questions or a request to delete Law Alerts information, email ukrightsradar@gmail.com.

Law Alerts beta

If you successfully register for the Law Alerts beta, RightsRadar UK stores the email address you provide together with your chosen jurisdiction, topics, alert-frequency preference and the time you gave consent. These details are used only to operate and develop the Law Alerts service. The signup does not ask for details about a legal case.

Law Alerts registration data is intended to be stored in private server-side storage connected to the RightsRadar UK Vercel project. If that storage is unavailable, the signup form will tell you that your preferences could not be saved rather than silently collecting them elsewhere.

Automated email delivery is not live yet. The beta registration page makes this clear and no paid Law Alerts subscription is offered until the delivery system is connected.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting services, Vercel may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page, time and security-related logs.

Cookies, consent and advertising

RightsRadar UK is preparing Google AdSense and has selected Google’s consent-management platform for UK/EEA consent. Advertising is not treated as active merely because the site is under AdSense review. This notice and the site’s consent controls will be kept aligned with the advertising configuration before non-essential advertising storage or personalised advertising is enabled.

Live law feed

The New Laws page asks the RightsRadar UK server to retrieve public information from legislation.gov.uk and the UK Parliament. Those public feeds are not sent your Law Alerts email address or preferences.

Why information is used and how long it is kept

Law Alerts beta information is processed because you actively ask to register and consent to the storage of the preferences shown on the form. Technical hosting and security information may be processed where necessary for the legitimate interests of operating, securing and diagnosing the website.

Law Alerts beta information will be removed on a valid deletion request and will be reviewed when the beta ends or moves to a full service. RightsRadar UK will not keep beta registrations indefinitely without a continuing service purpose. Infrastructure-log retention is controlled through the hosting service and its applicable settings.

Sharing and service providers

RightsRadar UK does not sell Law Alerts registration data. Service providers may process information where needed to host the website, store preferences, deliver future emails or process future payments. When those services are activated, this notice will identify material changes to the way personal information is handled.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Visit ico.org.uk for the official complaint route.

Changes to this notice

This page will be updated when the website’s data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
