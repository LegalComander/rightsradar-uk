Privacy notice

This notice explains how RightsRadar UK handles information when you use this website. RightsRadar UK is an independent legal-information project. For privacy questions, email ukrightsradar@gmail.com.

Information the current website uses

The current website does not create user accounts or send the email address entered in the law-alert demonstration to a server. If you use that demonstration, the preference is stored in your browser’s local storage on that device. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting services, Vercel may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page, time and security-related logs.

Cookies and analytics

RightsRadar UK does not currently add advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising, accounts or real email alerts are introduced, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated before those features are used.

Live law feed

The New Laws page asks the RightsRadar UK server to retrieve public information from legislation.gov.uk and the UK Parliament. Those public feeds do not receive the email address entered in the demonstration form.

Legal basis and retention

Technical hosting and security information may be processed where necessary for the legitimate interests of operating, securing and diagnosing the website. Retention of infrastructure logs is controlled through the hosting service and its applicable settings. Device-local demonstration preferences remain on your device until you remove them.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Visit ico.org.uk for the official complaint route.

Changes to this notice

This page will be updated when the website’s data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
